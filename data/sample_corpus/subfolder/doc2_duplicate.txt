Text preprocessing is an essential first phase in building information retrieval and search systems.
During preprocessing, raw text documents are loaded safely from storage, normalized across character encodings, and sanitized.

Important steps include:
- Unicode normalization (e.g., NFC format)
- Deduplication of identical documents via cryptographic hashes
- Splitting continuous text streams into overlapping chunks
- Extracting metadata such as timestamps, file sizes, and character offsets

Proper chunking ensures that embedding models and ranking algorithms operate on coherent passages without truncating critical context.
